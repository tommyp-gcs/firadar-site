How the FiRadar Score Works

The FiRadar Score measures fintech readiness and proof of execution on a scale of 0–1,000. The higher the score, the more likely a larger financial institution would purchase the solution.

Our AI scoring engine analyzes each company's public presence across six weighted dimensions, benchmarking against world-class leaders like Chime, SoFi, Revolut, Stripe, and Plaid.

Scores are generated programmatically via the FiRadar API — consistent, repeatable, and independent.

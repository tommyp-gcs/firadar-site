Key distinctions for credit unions

Not every digital dollar solution is the same. Know which one your vendor is offering.

Tokenized deposits

Stay on the CU balance sheet, inside the insured perimeter. An efficiency play: faster settlement and programmable money without leaving the regulated deposit framework.

Stablecoins

1:1 reserve-backed and move more freely across networks. The GENIUS Act names insured CUs as permitted issuers, but balance sheet and regulatory implications differ.

Banks as frenemies

The biggest tokenized deposit players (JPMorgan Kinexys, The Clearing House) are banks. They may offer infrastructure, but they also compete for your members’ deposits.

Regulatory timing

NCUA rules implementing the GENIUS Act were due July 2026. Confirm current status before committing. Today’s pilot may be production-ready by mid-2027.
